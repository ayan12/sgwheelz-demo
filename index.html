Car Trading Management System

Run your entire used-car business

SGWheelz is a complete web-based system for buying, selling and exporting used cars — tracking every deal, calculating profit and commission automatically, and generating professional invoices. Built to replace messy spreadsheets, usable by your whole team from any device.

Works on any phone or computer Secure & encrypted Automatic daily backups
What it is

A proper system, not another spreadsheet

Every car you buy and sell is logged once — the dealer, the buyer, the prices, tow fees and status. The system instantly works out your balance and commission, produces branded PDF invoices, keeps a contact book of dealers and buyers, and turns it all into clear monthly reports. One source of truth your whole team can trust.

Everything included

What the system does

Deal tracking

Log each car once. Balance and commission calculate automatically as you type.

Professional invoices

One-click PDF purchase & sale invoices, branded and ready to send to dealers and buyers.

Dealers & buyers

A built-in contact book, with totals showing how much each buyer represents.

Reports & Excel

Monthly summaries and totals by buyer, with one-click Excel and CSV export.

One-step Excel import

Bring your existing records straight in, with a safe preview before anything is saved.

Commission tracking

Mark commissions paid or unpaid and see, at a glance, exactly what you're still owed.

Roles & permissions

Owner and staff logins. Staff enter daily data but can't delete records or change settings.

Activity log & bulk tools

See who changed what and when — and edit, update or clear many records at once.
